The problem

When a renewal is missed, services can stop.

Families

Track benefit deadlines on paper, in email, and from memory.

Caregivers and staff

Notes, hours, and paperwork are scattered across forms and inboxes.

Agencies

Compliance gets checked after the fact, usually at audit time.

Everyone works from different information. Nobody has the full picture.

How it works

One renewal, caught before it lapses.

Benefit status tracking and renewal reminders, shared by the whole circle around each person.

1Agency

Marcus is flagged

His Medi-Cal renewal is due in 21 days and the form isn't signed. His pay stubs are already attached from payroll, so nobody chases paperwork.

2Caregiver

Lena gets a task

Help Marcus review and sign his renewal form by Friday. Marcus makes the decisions. Lena helps him understand the form.

3Client

Marcus stays in the loop

He sees his own update in plain words, and he decides what his family can see.

Built for adults

The person stays in control.

Most tools in this space are built for parents of kids. Almova is built for adults, and for supported decision-making: the person keeps their decisions and picks who helps.

Consent first

Adult clients decide what family sees, and can change it anytime.

Their own circle

Living independently? They can invite a friend, job coach, or mentor, or no one.

A safety net

If a deadline goes quiet, Almova alerts their coordinator so a person reaches out.

Almova Vault

A portable record the person and their family own. Share it with a new doctor, agency, or employer with a QR code, and see who opened it.

Privacy and responsible AI

Designed for HIPAA from day one.

Locked down by role

Encryption, minimum-necessary access, and an audit log of every view.

Business Associate Agreements

We sign them with Regional Centers and vendors, the same way Ultra Services does today. Cloud and AI providers sign them with us too.

AI that waits for approval

Core tracking and reminders run without generative AI. AI features switch on in the Regional Center system as DDS approves them.

A person approves every decision

AI never decides eligibility on its own. It suggests, and a person confirms.
